How old is David Ortiz?

How tall is David Ortiz?

David Ortiz is 6-3 (190 cm) tall.

How much did David Ortiz weigh when playing?

David Ortiz played 20 seasons.

David Ortiz was elected into the Hall of Fame in 2022.
